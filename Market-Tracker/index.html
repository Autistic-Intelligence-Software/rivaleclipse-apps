Predictions are probabilities built from pattern statistics — barrier distance to your line, volatility, momentum, and each coin's 6-month base rates. They are not guarantees, and short-horizon crypto direction is close to a coin flip: the base models' held-out record is printed on every card, and the scoreboard above shows the system's global record from the shared database — the same numbers every visitor sees, wins and losses alike. The snipe badge uses the hourly setup only, read on a ~4-hour window: on held-out data across all 20 coins, flagged setups produced the big move 23.1% of the time against a 14.4% unflagged base rate (1.61x lift). The 15-minute snipe tested as noise (lift under 1x) and is not used. Each card's forecast section stays collapsed until you open it — while it is closed, the page runs no forecast rendering or forecast data work at all. Inside it, the Estimate graph is a live prediction feed: the system's current predicted path — built from the coin's own recent swing rhythm, leg durations and sizes measured from the last two hours of 1-minute prices — flows in from the right and scrolls left past the live price bead, which stays centered and leaves a trail behind it. Drawn beside the real trail is the comparison line: what the system predicted for each past moment 1, 5 or 10 minutes before it happened (the graph's chips pick the age), so prediction and reality can be watched tracking or diverging in plain sight. It is a rhythm-matched estimate, not a promised path — and the scoreboard of record for these predictions is the system's own graded forecast-check record, kept on the server, not this picture. All tuning lives in one shared database on the server: the system scores its own predictions as they close and retunes separate weights for momentum, regime, and rise/fall asymmetry; confidence buckets that run hot get shrunk toward 50; a coin whose calls keep flip-flopping gets a wider no-call band and a steadier, dwell-held call; and the volatility inside every probability follows the last few hours of live market, blended with the 6-month base. This page only pulls the current global tuning as it loads and refreshes — each card's Global line shows the shared record it is running on. The Trend read on each call is a plain-language summary of those same pattern signals — trend state, how rises have been running against falls, swing timing, forecast slope, and this coin's hour-of-day habits over the 6-month history — read off the numbers at that moment, not a separate prediction and not a guarantee. The forecast-check graph freezes when you open it and zooms into the forecast's first 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes: the actual price then draws over the frozen forecast minute by minute, with the system's measured error printed on the graph — so you can see exactly how close the prediction is tracking, and whether it is catching the trend shifts as they happen. The forecast-check graph draws the system's minute-by-minute predicted path — built from the coin's own recent swing rhythm (leg durations and sizes measured from the last two hours of 1-minute prices), waved forward and anchored to the system's drift expectation at the 30-minute mark. Still a rhythm-matched estimate, not a promised path. The system runs its own forecast checks on the server around the clock: it draws this same predicted path for every coin, grades it against the real price at 5, 10, 15 and 30 minutes as the results happen, and logs every result in the shared database that tunes future forecasts. This page only displays that record — there is no account and no sign-in, nothing is uploaded from your browser, and nothing about your visit is saved or used for learning.
