Perpetual Helper

Live prices for the cryptos you pick, a cycle timer synced to the market clock, and an over/under call for the close from 6 months of pattern stats per coin. Line & Position Pad

Calculator-style entry: pick the crypto, tap your target line in on the big pad (or type — your hardware keyboard works while this panel is in use), tell it which side you hold with Up / Down, then hit ENTER. It runs the page's own prediction math — the same global params the watch cards run on — for your coin and your line at every standard horizon, and tells you plainly what the numbers say about your position, including what flipping or cashing out does to them. Advisory only: it never places trades, and probabilities are not guarantees.

Predictions are probabilities built from pattern statistics — barrier distance to your line, volatility, momentum, and each coin's 6-month base rates. They are not guarantees, and short-horizon crypto direction is close to a coin flip: the base models' held-out record is printed on every card, and the scoreboard above shows the system's global record from the shared database — the same numbers every visitor sees, wins and losses alike. The snipe badge uses the hourly setup only, read on a ~4-hour window: on held-out data across all 20 coins, flagged setups produced the big move 23.1% of the time against a 14.4% unflagged base rate (1.61x lift). The 15-minute snipe tested as noise (lift under 1x) and is not used. The forecast chart beside each live chart is an estimate cone — the bands show where the price could plausibly wander given current volatility, and the mean path inside them is built from the coin's own recent swing rhythm: the durations and sizes of its real up and down legs, measured from the last two hours of 1-minute prices and waved forward over the system's drift. It is a rhythm-matched estimate, not a promised path; the tick marks are the times swings have typically arrived, not appointments. All tuning lives in one shared database on the server: the system scores its own predictions as they close and retunes separate weights for momentum, regime, and rise/fall asymmetry; confidence buckets that run hot get shrunk toward 50; a coin whose calls keep flip-flopping gets a wider no-call band and a steadier, dwell-held call; and the volatility inside every probability follows the last few hours of live market, blended with the 6-month base. This page only pulls the current global tuning as it loads and refreshes — each card's Global line shows the shared record it is running on. The Trend read on each call is a plain-language summary of those same pattern signals — trend state, how rises have been running against falls, swing timing, forecast slope, and this coin's hour-of-day habits over the 6-month history — read off the numbers at that moment, not a separate prediction and not a guarantee. The forecast-check graph freezes when you open it and zooms into the forecast's first 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes: the actual price then draws over the frozen forecast minute by minute, with the system's measured error printed on the graph — so you can see exactly how close the prediction is tracking, and whether it is catching the trend shifts as they happen. The forecast-check graph draws the system's minute-by-minute predicted path — built from the coin's own recent swing rhythm (leg durations and sizes measured from the last two hours of 1-minute prices), waved forward and anchored to the system's drift expectation at the 30-minute mark. Still a rhythm-matched estimate, not a promised path. The system runs its own forecast checks on the server around the clock: it draws this same predicted path for every coin, grades it against the real price at 5, 10, 15 and 30 minutes as the results happen, and logs every result in the shared database that tunes future forecasts. This page only displays that record — there is no account and no sign-in, nothing is uploaded from your browser, and nothing about your visit is saved or used for learning.
